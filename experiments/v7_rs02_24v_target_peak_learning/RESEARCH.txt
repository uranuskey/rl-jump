Research checked 2026-10-06, primary sources.
Schulman et al., Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms (2017): https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347 . PPO optimizes a surrogate objective through sampled interactions and minibatch updates. This experiment keeps the existing episodic clipped PPO implementation, decreases actor LR, tightens its empirical KL guard, and evaluates after each update. The paper does not establish that our values or first-contact peak will improve.
Achiam et al., Constrained Policy Optimization (2017): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a.html . Rewards and constraints are modeled separately; CPO's near-constraint guarantees belong to its algorithm and assumptions, not our modified PPO.
Yang et al., Accelerating Safe Reinforcement Learning with Constraint-mismatched Baseline Policies (2021): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/yang21i.html . A baseline may violate constraints; their approach uses reward improvement, baseline distance and projection. We do not implement their projection method.
Our native worst-force learning allowance0.05%,LR2.5e-6,KL0.005 and single-update chunk are declared experiment settings, not theoretical/hardware bounds. All final gates stay fixed. The source remains a failed actor until a changed learned controller passes native45+three512. No matched control proves which intervention causes any outcome.
