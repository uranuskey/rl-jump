Primary sources checked2026-10-05:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/portelas20a.html
Portelas et al., Teacher algorithms for curriculum learning of Deep RL in
continuously parameterized environments, CoRL/PMLR2020. The paper studies
selecting task parameters according to learning progress and learnability.
We borrow the curriculum principle, not its ALP-GMM algorithm: practice at a
qualified frontier before retesting a harder task with an updated policy.

https://proceedings.mlr.press/v164/rudin22a.html
Rudin et al., Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep RL,
CoRL/PMLR2022. Parallel legged training with a game-inspired curriculum supports
progressive difficulty rather than directly training only the hardest tasks.
Our robot, jump task and gate/physics setup differ; their runtime and hardware
claims cannot be transferred. Two warmup updates and35% are our bounded experiment,
not parameters prescribed by either paper. All existing gates remain unchanged.
