These sources motivate testing timing and touchdown motion. The actual choice27.5/25/20ms is a
bounded project experiment justified by the preserved 30/35/40/45ms native traces, not a parameter
prescribed by either paper. The existing budget taper correlates with lower contraction command
speed near contact for longer horizons. This is not proof of causal peak reduction for shorter
horizons. No full cited algorithm, theoretical guarantee or hardware validation is claimed.
